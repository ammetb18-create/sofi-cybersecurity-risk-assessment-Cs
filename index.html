SoFi Technologies Cybersecurity Risk Assessment

Independent GRC Analysis Based on Publicly Available Information

A comprehensive cybersecurity governance, risk, and compliance case study demonstrating practical expertise in risk assessment, control mapping, audit readiness, and executive-level security documentation for a financial technology company.

Project Overview

Case Study Purpose

This independent case study demonstrates practical skills in cybersecurity governance, risk management, control mapping, audit readiness, and executive-level security documentation. The analysis identifies key cybersecurity risk areas relevant to fintech companies, including access control, third-party/vendor risk, cloud and data protection, incident response readiness, monitoring, audit evidence, and regulatory exposure.

Business Context

SoFi operates in the financial technology sector, where digital platforms, customer trust, sensitive financial data, third-party providers, regulatory expectations, and system availability are central to business operations. A cybersecurity failure could potentially affect customer trust, regulatory compliance, business operations, financial reporting, vendor relationships, brand reputation, and legal exposure.

GRC Analysis Scope

This case study focuses on cybersecurity risk assessment, access control and identity management, third-party and vendor risk, cloud and data protection, incident response readiness, security monitoring and audit logging, control mapping, remediation planning, and executive-level reporting—all from the perspective of an entry-level cybersecurity GRC analyst using only publicly available information.
