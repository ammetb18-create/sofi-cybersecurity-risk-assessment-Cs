# Cybersecurity Risk Matrix

## Purpose

This risk matrix provides a visual summary of the cybersecurity risks identified in the risk register. It organizes risks by likelihood and impact to help prioritize remediation efforts from a GRC analyst perspective.

The goal of this matrix is to support risk-based decision-making, executive communication, and remediation planning.

## Risk Matrix Scale

| Impact / Likelihood | Low Likelihood | Medium Likelihood | High Likelihood |
|---|---|---|---|
| High Impact | High | High | Critical |
| Medium Impact | Medium | Medium | High |
| Low Impact | Low | Medium | Medium |

## Critical Risks

The following risks are rated as Critical because they combine high likelihood with high business, customer, regulatory, or operational impact:

| Risk ID | Critical Risk | Business Concern |
|---|---|---|
| R-001 | Unauthorized access to customer personal or financial data | Could affect customer trust, privacy, regulatory exposure, and financial services integrity. |
| R-002 | Phishing attack targeting employees or administrative users | Could lead to credential compromise, unauthorized access, and operational disruption. |
| R-010 | Account takeover affecting customer accounts | Could result in fraud, unauthorized activity, customer harm, and reputational damage. |

## High Risks

The following risks are rated as High because they have significant potential impact and should be actively managed:

| Risk ID | High Risk | Business Concern |
|---|---|---|
| R-003 | Excessive employee access privileges | Could allow unnecessary or inappropriate access to sensitive systems and customer data. |
| R-004 | Weak offboarding process | Could leave active access for former employees or contractors. |
| R-005 | Third-party vendor security weakness or data breach | Could create indirect data exposure, compliance issues, or service disruption. |
| R-006 | Misconfigured cloud storage | Could expose sensitive files or internal documentation. |
| R-007 | Incomplete security logging or monitoring | Could reduce the organization’s ability to detect, investigate, and respond to incidents. |
| R-008 | Incomplete incident response documentation or testing | Could delay response during a real cybersecurity event. |
| R-009 | Insecure handling of loan application documents | Could expose sensitive customer-submitted financial and identity information. |
| R-012 | Regulatory or compliance exposure due to inadequate cybersecurity governance | Could affect audit readiness, regulatory response, and cybersecurity program maturity. |

## Medium Risks

| Risk ID | Medium Risk | Business Concern |
|---|---|---|
| R-011 | Weak audit evidence management | Could make audits less efficient and reduce confidence in control effectiveness. |

## Risk Prioritization Summary

Based on the matrix, the most urgent remediation focus should be placed on:

1. Strengthening access controls and authentication
2. Reducing phishing and credential compromise risk
3. Improving customer account protection
4. Managing third-party and vendor exposure
5. Improving cloud and data protection controls
6. Strengthening logging, monitoring, and incident response readiness

## GRC Analyst Takeaway

The risk matrix helps translate technical and operational cybersecurity concerns into a business-readable prioritization model.

For a GRC analyst, this type of matrix supports:

- Executive reporting
- Control prioritization
- Remediation planning
- Risk ownership discussions
- Audit readiness
- Compliance communication
