Critical Risks (3)

These risks combine high likelihood with high business, customer, regulatory, or operational impact:

  • R-001 - Unauthorized access to customer data: Could affect customer trust, privacy, and regulatory exposure
  • R-002 - Phishing attacks: Could lead to credential compromise and operational disruption
  • R-010 - Account takeover: Could result in fraud, unauthorized activity, and reputational damage

Risk Prioritization Summary

Based on the matrix, the most urgent remediation focus should address:

  1. Strengthening access controls and authentication
  2. Reducing phishing and credential compromise risk
  3. Improving customer account protection
  4. Managing third-party and vendor exposure
  5. Improving cloud and data protection controls
  6. Strengthening logging, monitoring, and incident response readiness
