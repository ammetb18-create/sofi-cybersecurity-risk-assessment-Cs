Highest Priority Risks

The highest priority risks are prioritized because they directly affect customer trust, sensitive financial information, regulatory exposure, business continuity, and the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of digital financial services:

  1. R-001: Unauthorized access to customer personal or financial data
  2. R-002: Phishing attacks targeting employees or administrative users
  3. R-010: Account takeover affecting customer accounts
  4. R-005: Third-party vendor security weakness or data breach
  5. R-006: Misconfigured cloud storage exposing sensitive files
